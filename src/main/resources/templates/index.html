Get started in three steps

  1. Explore the endpoints

    Open Swagger UI to see available operations and payloads.

  2. Create an account

    Register with POST /users, then sign in with POST /auth/login.

  3. Use your access token

    Send Authorization: Bearer <token> for protected operations. Cart endpoints are public.
